🚀 Week 5 - Client-Server Technologies

This week, I designed and developed **Neko Wave Cafe**, a responsive Laravel landing page and mini business application for a cat cafe. I used Laravel, Blade Components, Tailwind CSS, and responsive design principles to create a warm, mobile-friendly experience with a menu, cat profiles, a reservation form, and a contact form.

I focused on reusable components, clearer visual hierarchy, and the small interaction details that make a business site feel inviting. Comparing the early content-first wireframe with the final interface showed me how much thoughtful spacing, typography, and component structure can improve usability and maintainability.
